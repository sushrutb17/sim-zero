# How to try Sim Zero

About ten minutes, seated, hands only.

## What you need

- A Meta Quest headset with **hand tracking** turned on. Put the controllers down; you won't need them.
- A chair. Sim Zero is designed to be used seated.
- The headset volume turned up: every radio, cabin and first-officer line is spoken.

The hosted build runs in the **Meta Quest Browser**. It does not run as a VR experience in a desktop browser. It has been tested on a Meta Quest 2.

## 1. Start (under a minute)

1. In the Meta Quest Browser, open **https://vr-cockpit-trainer.vercel.app**.
2. Press **Start Sim Zero**. That one press turns on the sound and takes you into the cockpit.
3. Allow hand tracking and immersive mode if the browser asks.

If you entered with the browser's own **Enter VR** button instead and the welcome screen says **Sound is off**, pinch any button to turn it on.

## 2. Your first flight: Single Source, Guided Flight (about 7 minutes)

1. On **Welcome to Sim Zero**, adjust the seat if the panels feel too close or too far: **SEAT FWD** and **SEAT BACK** move it from 0 to +50 cm. Then press **CONTINUE**.
2. At **Choose a flight**, pick **Single Source** (a generator fails over open water).
3. At **How do you want to fly?**, pick **GUIDED FLIGHT**. It shows each step.
4. **Quick practice:** look at the kneeboard on your left and press its **PRACTICE** switch once. That is the only way past this screen.
5. **Briefing:** read it on the left kneeboard (**NEXT** and **PREV** turn the pages), then press **BEGIN**.
6. Follow the instruction strip in the windscreen and the **violet frame** around the next control to press. The flight has four parts: before takeoff, takeoff and climb, the failure, and your decision. At the decision every option is framed, and the choice is yours.

## 3. The debrief (about 2 minutes)

1. Read the grade and the reasons for each part of the flight.
2. On the generator-failure row, press **TIMELINE**. It shows what arrived and when you answered, and the electrical system at the start, at the fault and at the end. **BACK** returns to the grades.
3. Optional: press **REDO** on any row to fly that part again, then compare the **FIRST** and **REDO** grades.
4. Press **FINISH** when you're done.

## Using your hands

- **Poke:** move a fingertip into a button, then pull it back before pressing again.
- **Point and pinch:** point at a target from a distance and pinch to select it.
- Make separate, deliberate presses. After a screen changes, take your hand away before pressing the new screen.
- **Guarded switches** (such as BUS TIE) take two presses: the first lifts the cover, the second throws the switch.
- Ticked a checklist line by mistake? Poke it again to untick it. There's no penalty.
- You answer the controller, the cabin and dispatch by pressing **reply cards**. Your replies are not spoken.

## Try more

From the debrief, press **RESTART** to return to **Choose a flight**.

- **PRACTISE ALONE:** the same flights with no instruction strip and no frames, graded as flown. On Single Source it draws one of twelve variations.
- **Time Critical** (about 7 minutes): a passenger collapses in cruise. No system fails; you gather facts from the cabin, choose an airport and brief the crew.
- **Cross-Check** (about 6 minutes): a late change to the departure and a busy frequency, ahead of a cabin call.

When two messages are waiting, the comms panel shows the newest. Tap its tab (**ATC**, **CABIN** or **DISPATCH**) to reach the earlier one.

## If something goes wrong

- **Need a break:** press **PAUSE** on the kneeboard, then **RESUME**.
- **Want to start over:** on the paused screen, **RESTART** (top right) returns to **Choose a flight**.
- **Stuck on Quick practice:** press the actual **PRACTICE** switch on the kneeboard, not the text on the screen.
- **A press doesn't register:** take your hand away, let the screen settle, and try a deliberate poke or a point-and-pinch.
- **No sound:** check the headset volume. If you entered with **Enter VR**, pinch any button once.

---

Sim Zero is a rehearsal prototype, not certified training. The aircraft, airline, airports and every procedure are fictional and were invented for this project.
